About the Conference

The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applications (NCAIA 2027) is organized by the Research Laboratory in Computer Science of Sidi Bel Abbès (LabRI-SBA) at the École Supérieure en Informatique (ESI-SBA). The conference provides a national forum for researchers, academics, industry professionals, and graduate students to present recent advances, present research findings, and discuss emerging challenges in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Applications.

NCAIA 2027 particularly encourages research addressing major national priorities through innovative Artificial Intelligence and computing solutions.

AI for National Priorities

NCAIA 2027 particularly welcomes research applying Artificial Intelligence to major national challenges.

Food Security

Artificial Intelligence for agriculture, precision farming, crop monitoring, disease detection, food production, forecasting, irrigation, and food supply chains.
