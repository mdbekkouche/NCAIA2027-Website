Call for Papers

1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing

April 11–12, 2027 | ESI-SBA, Sidi Bel Abbès, Algeria

Call for Papers

The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing (NCAIAC 2027) invites researchers, academics, engineers, practitioners, and graduate students to submit original and unpublished research papers addressing advances in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing.

The conference aims to provide a national scientific forum for presenting research results, innovative methodologies, computational approaches, practical applications, and emerging technologies.

Particular attention is given to research that applies Artificial Intelligence and computing technologies to major national challenges, including food security, citizen health, energy security, and cybersecurity.

Priority Application Areas

NCAIAC 2027 particularly encourages submissions addressing the following strategic application areas.

Food Security

AI and Applied Computing for agriculture and food systems, including smart agriculture, precision agriculture, crop monitoring, disease detection, yield prediction, agricultural forecasting, smart irrigation, food quality, supply-chain optimization, and intelligent farming systems.

Citizen Health

AI and Applied Computing for healthcare and public health, including disease prediction, medical imaging, clinical decision support, health-risk assessment, digital health, remote monitoring, bioinformatics, medical signal processing, and healthcare analytics.

Energy Security

AI and Applied Computing for energy systems, including renewable energy forecasting, energy demand prediction, smart grids, energy optimization, energy management, predictive maintenance, fault detection, energy efficiency, and intelligent energy infrastructure.

Cybersecurity

AI and Applied Computing for cybersecurity, including intrusion detection, log event detection, malware detection, cyber-threat intelligence, security analytics, digital forensics, adversarial machine learning, privacy-preserving AI, IoT security, cloud security, and protection of critical infrastructures.

Submission Requirements

Authors are invited to submit original research papers written in English. Submitted manuscripts must not have been previously published or be under review elsewhere.

Each submission will undergo peer review by members of the Scientific Committee. Papers should clearly describe the research problem, methodology, experimental or theoretical results, and scientific contribution.

  • Language: English
  • Paper Length: 6–10 pages
  • Format: NCAIAC 2027 LaTeX Template
  • Submission: PDF
  • Review: Peer Review
  • Originality: Original and unpublished work
