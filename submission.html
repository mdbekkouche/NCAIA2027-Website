1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applications

April 11–12, 2027 | ESI-SBA, Sidi Bel Abbès, Algeria

Submission Information

The National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applications (NCAIA 2027) invites researchers, academics, engineers, practitioners, and graduate students to submit original and unpublished research papers in Artificial Intelligence, and related disciplines.

NCAIA 2027 particularly encourages contributions applying Artificial Intelligence and computing technologies to major national challenges, including:

Food Security

Smart agriculture, food systems, crop monitoring, prediction, optimization, and intelligent farming.

Citizen Health

Healthcare AI, disease prediction, medical imaging, digital health, and clinical decision support.

Energy Security

Smart energy systems, forecasting, optimization, renewable energy, and predictive maintenance.

Cybersecurity

Intrusion detection, log event detection, threat intelligence, privacy, and critical infrastructure security.

Submission Requirements

Authors should ensure that their manuscripts meet the following requirements:

  • The paper must present original and unpublished research.
  • The manuscript must be written in English.
  • The paper should contain approximately 6–10 pages.
  • The manuscript must follow the NCAIA 2027 LaTeX template.
  • The submitted manuscript must be in PDF format.
  • All figures and tables must be properly embedded in the manuscript.
  • Figures and tables must be numbered and referenced in the text.
  • References should follow the required IEEE citation style.
  • The manuscript must not be simultaneously submitted to another conference or journal.

Peer Review Process

Each submitted paper will undergo peer review coordinated by the NCAIA 2027 Scientific Committee. Reviewers will assess submissions according to criteria including:

  • Originality
  • Scientific contribution
  • Technical quality
  • Methodological rigor
  • Experimental or theoretical validation
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Relevance to the conference scope

Submission Checklist

  • ☐ The paper is written in English.
  • ☐ The manuscript follows the NCAIA 2027 template.
  • ☐ The paper complies with the page-length requirements.
  • ☐ The paper presents original and unpublished research.
  • ☐ Figures and tables are numbered and cited.
  • ☐ References follow the required citation style.
  • ☐ The PDF has been carefully checked before submission.
  • ☐ The paper is not under review elsewhere.
