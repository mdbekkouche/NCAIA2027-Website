The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing

Scope

NCAIAC 2027 welcomes original research contributions describing theoretical advances, innovative methodologies, practical applications, experimental studies, and industrial experiences in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing. The conference encourages interdisciplinary research and welcomes papers covering, but not limited to, the following topics.

Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit original and unpublished research papers related to one or more of the conference topics. Submissions should follow the official NCAIAC 2027 formatting guidelines and be prepared using the conference LaTeX template.
