Call for Papers

The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing

Conference Overview

The National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing (NCAIAC 2027) invites researchers, academics, engineers, practitioners, and graduate students to submit original and unpublished research papers. The conference aims to provide a national forum for presenting recent advances, innovative methodologies, practical applications, and emerging trends in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing. Accepted papers will be presented during the conference and included in the official conference proceedings.

Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit original research papers written in English. Submitted manuscripts must not have been previously published or be under review elsewhere. Each submission will undergo a peer-review process conducted by members of the Scientific Committee.

  • Language: English
  • Paper Length: 6–10 pages
  • Format: Official NCAIAC LaTeX Template
  • Submission Format: PDF
  • Review Process: Peer Review

Publication
