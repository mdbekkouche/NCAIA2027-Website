About the Conference

The National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing (NCAIAC) is an annual scientific event organized by the Research Laboratory in Computer Science of Sidi Bel Abbès (LabRI-SBA) at the École Supérieure en Informatique (ESI-SBA). The conference provides a national forum for researchers, academics, industry professionals, and graduate students to present recent advances, exchange ideas, and discuss emerging challenges in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Applied Computing.
