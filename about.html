About NCAIAC 2027

National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing

Conference Overview

The National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing (NCAIAC 2027) is a scientific forum dedicated to promoting research, innovation, and collaboration in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing. The conference brings together researchers, academics, industry professionals, engineers, and graduate students to present recent scientific advances, exchange ideas, discuss emerging technologies, and strengthen collaborations within the national research community. NCAIAC encourages interdisciplinary research and provides an environment where participants can present original research, practical applications, and innovative solutions addressing current scientific and industrial challenges.

Conference Objectives

  • Promote high-quality scientific research in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing.
  • Provide a forum for exchanging recent research results.
  • Encourage collaboration between universities, research laboratories, and industry.
  • Support young researchers and graduate students.
  • Strengthen the national Artificial Intelligence research community.
  • Present innovative AI applications addressing real-world problems.
  • Encourage multidisciplinary research.
  • Promote technology transfer between academia and industry.
  • Facilitate networking among researchers.
  • Promote future national and international collaborations.

Conference Scope

NCAIAC welcomes original research contributions covering theoretical foundations, computational methods, innovative algorithms, practical applications, and emerging technologies in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing. The conference covers a broad spectrum of research domains, including Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Robotics, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Intelligent Systems, Optimization, Internet of Things, Smart Cities, Healthcare AI, Bioinformatics, High-Performance Computing, Cloud Computing, Software Engineering, and Decision Support Systems.

Who Should Attend?

Researchers

Faculty members, scientists, and research engineers working in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing.

Students

Master's students, PhD candidates, and young researchers wishing to present their work and receive expert feedback.

Industry Professionals

Engineers, practitioners, entrepreneurs, and technology companies interested in Artificial Intelligence applications.

Conference Venue

NCAIAC 2027 will be held at the École Supérieure en Informatique (ESI-SBA) located in Sidi Bel Abbès, Algeria. Participants may attend either in person or remotely through the conference's hybrid participation system.
