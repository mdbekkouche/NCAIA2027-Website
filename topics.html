1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applications

Conference Scope

NCAIA 2027 welcomes original research contributions covering theoretical foundations, innovative methodologies, computational approaches, experimental studies, practical applications, and emerging technologies in Artificial Intelligence and its Applications.

The conference has a broad scientific scope. In particular, NCAIA 2027 encourages research addressing major national priorities through Artificial Intelligence and computing technologies.

Priority application areas: Food Security, Citizen Health, Energy Security, and Cybersecurity.

The topics listed below are indicative and are not exhaustive. Papers addressing related subjects within Artificial Intelligence and its Applications are also welcome.
