Paper Submission

The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing

Submission Information

The National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing (NCAIAC 2027) invites authors to submit original and unpublished research papers describing significant contributions in Artificial Intelligence, Applied Computing, and related disciplines. Submitted manuscripts must not have been previously published or be under review for publication elsewhere. All accepted papers will be presented during the conference.

Submission System

Paper submissions will be managed through the Microsoft CMT Conference Management Toolkit. Authors must create a CMT account before submitting their manuscripts.

Submit Your Paper (CMT submission link will be available soon.)

Submission Requirements

  • Original and unpublished work.
  • Written in English.
  • Paper length: 6–10 pages.
  • Prepared using the NCAIAC 2027 LaTeX template.
  • Submitted in PDF format.
  • The PDF must be clear, readable, and printable.
  • All figures and tables must be embedded in the manuscript.
  • References must follow the IEEE citation style.

Peer Review Process

Each submission will undergo a peer-review process coordinated by the Scientific Committee. Reviewers will evaluate manuscripts according to the following criteria:

  • Originality
  • Scientific quality
  • Technical soundness
  • Methodological rigor
  • Experimental validation
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Relevance to the conference topics

Submission Checklist

  • ☐ The paper is written in English.
  • ☐ The manuscript follows the official NCAIAC template.
  • ☐ The paper contains between 6 and 10 pages.
  • ☐ Figures and tables are numbered and cited.
  • ☐ Mathematical equations are properly formatted.
  • ☐ References follow the IEEE style.
  • ☐ The PDF file has been carefully checked before submission.
