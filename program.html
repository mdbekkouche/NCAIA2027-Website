Conference Program

The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing
April 11–12, 2027
École Supérieure en Informatique (ESI-SBA), Sidi Bel Abbès, Algeria

Program Overview

NCAIAC 2027 will bring together researchers, academics, engineers, graduate students, and professionals to present recent advances in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing. The program will combine keynote lectures, technical paper presentations, thematic sessions, discussions, and opportunities for scientific exchange. Particular attention will be given to Artificial Intelligence applications addressing major national and societal challenges, including food security, citizen health, energy security, and cybersecurity.

Program status: The detailed technical program, including paper titles, authors, presentation times, session chairs, and keynote speakers, will be published after the paper review and acceptance process.

Strategic Application Themes

In addition to methodological contributions in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing, the conference particularly encourages research addressing the following strategic application areas:

AI for Food Security

  • Smart agriculture
  • Crop monitoring and yield prediction
  • Precision agriculture
  • Agricultural data analytics
  • Food supply-chain optimization
  • Early detection of agricultural risks

AI for Citizen Health

  • Medical diagnosis and decision support
  • Health risk prediction
  • Medical imaging
  • Digital health
  • Healthcare data analytics
  • Personalized and preventive healthcare

AI for Energy Security

  • Energy demand forecasting
  • Smart grids
  • Renewable energy management
  • Energy consumption optimization
  • Predictive maintenance of energy systems
  • Intelligent energy management

AI for Cybersecurity

  • Intrusion detection
  • Network security
  • Cyber-threat detection
  • Log event detection
  • Security analytics
  • Privacy and trustworthy AI
