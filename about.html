About NCAIAC 2027

1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing

Conference Overview

The 1st National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing (NCAIAC 2027) is a national scientific forum organized by the Research Laboratory in Computer Science of Sidi Bel Abbès (LabRI-SBA) at the École Supérieure en Informatique (ESI-SBA).

The conference brings together researchers, academics, engineers, industry professionals, graduate students, and other members of the scientific and technological community to present recent research, discuss emerging challenges, and explore innovative applications of Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing.

NCAIAC 2027 provides an opportunity to present original research, practical applications, experimental results, methodologies, and innovative solutions addressing scientific, technological, and real-world challenges.

Artificial Intelligence for National Priorities

NCAIAC 2027 particularly encourages research applying Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing to major national challenges.

Food Security

AI applications in agriculture, precision farming, crop monitoring, disease detection, agricultural forecasting, smart irrigation, food production, and food supply chains.

Citizen Health

AI applications in healthcare, medical diagnosis, medical imaging, disease prediction, health monitoring, digital health, and clinical decision support.

Energy Security

AI applications in renewable energy, energy forecasting, smart grids, energy optimization, consumption prediction, and predictive maintenance.

Cybersecurity

AI applications in intrusion detection, log event detection, malware analysis, threat intelligence, security analytics, and cyber defense.

These areas represent priority application domains within the broader scope of NCAIAC 2027. Contributions in other areas of Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing are also welcome.

Conference Objectives

  • Promote high-quality research in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing.
  • Provide a national forum for presenting recent scientific results.
  • Encourage interdisciplinary research and scientific interaction.
  • Support young researchers, graduate students, and early-career scientists.
  • Strengthen the national research community in Artificial Intelligence and related fields.
  • Promote innovative AI solutions to real-world and national challenges.
  • Encourage research addressing food security, citizen health, energy security, and cybersecurity.
  • Facilitate interaction between academia, research laboratories, and industry.
  • Encourage the development and dissemination of applied computing solutions.
  • Promote future scientific and technological partnerships.

Conference Scope

NCAIAC 2027 welcomes original contributions covering theoretical foundations, computational methods, algorithms, experimental studies, systems, practical applications, and emerging technologies in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Computing.

The conference covers a broad range of topics, including:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Computer Vision
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Data Science and Data Mining
  • Big Data Analytics
  • Robotics and Intelligent Systems
  • Cybersecurity
  • Healthcare and Medical AI
  • Energy and Smart Grids
  • AI for Agriculture and Food Systems
  • Internet of Things
  • Cloud and Edge Computing
  • Optimization and Decision Support
  • Software Engineering and Applied Computing

For the complete list of topics, please visit the Conference Topics page.

Who Should Attend?

Researchers

Faculty members, scientists, research engineers, and researchers working in Artificial Intelligence, Applied Computing, and related fields.

Students

Master's students, PhD candidates, and young researchers interested in presenting their work and engaging with the research community.

Industry Professionals

Engineers, practitioners, entrepreneurs, technology companies, and professionals interested in AI and its practical applications.

Conference Venue

NCAIAC 2027 will be held on April 11–12, 2027 at the École Supérieure en Informatique (ESI-SBA) in Sidi Bel Abbès, Algeria.

The conference will offer a hybrid participation format, allowing participants to attend either on site or online.

The venue provides an academic environment for scientific presentations, discussions, and interaction between participants.
