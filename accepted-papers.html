Review Process

All submitted manuscripts undergo a rigorous peer-review process conducted by members of the Scientific Committee. Each paper is evaluated according to originality, technical quality, significance, clarity of presentation, and relevance to the conference topics.

Only papers that successfully complete the review process and satisfy the reviewers' recommendations will be accepted for presentation at the conference.

Conference Proceedings

All accepted and presented papers will be included in the proceedings of the First National Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Applications (NCAIA 2027).

Additional information regarding the ISBN, DOI assignment (if applicable), and publication details will be announced before the conference.
